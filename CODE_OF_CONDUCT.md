# Code of Conduct

Portable Developer welcomes contributors and users regardless of experience, identity, background, or location.

## Expected behavior

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on the work;
- welcome questions and constructive disagreement;
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information;
- report security issues through the private channel described in `SECURITY.md`;
- do not harass, threaten, impersonate, or deliberately disrupt other participants.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/hybernia1). The maintainer may edit or remove abusive content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, subject to legal and safety obligations.

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, release discussions, and other project spaces.
